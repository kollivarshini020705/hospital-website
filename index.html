Medical Services & Tests

Comprehensive healthcare solutions tailored to your needs.

General Checkups

Comprehensive head-to-toe examinations. We assess vital signs, monitor chronic conditions, and provide preventive health strategies tailored to your age and lifestyle to ensure you stay in optimal condition year-round.

Laboratory Tests

State-of-the-art diagnostic testing with rapid turnaround times. We offer comprehensive blood work, pathology, hormone panels, and infectious disease screening using the latest medical technology for accurate diagnosis.

Radiology & Imaging

Advanced imaging center equipped with high-resolution MRI, CT scans, ultrasound, and digital X-rays. Our expert radiologists provide precise imaging necessary for complex surgical planning and accurate medical diagnosis.

Pharmacy Services

Fully stocked on-site pharmacy available 24/7. We offer prescription medications, over-the-counter remedies, and specialized healthcare supplies. Our pharmacists provide expert consultations on medication management.

Emergency Care

24/7 Level-1 trauma center ready for any critical situation. Our emergency medical technicians and trauma surgeons provide immediate, life-saving care for severe injuries, cardiac events, and acute illnesses.

Specialized Surgery

World-class surgical suites designed for minimally invasive, robotic, and complex open surgeries. Our top-tier surgical team specializes in orthopedics, neurosurgery, cardiovascular, and general surgical procedures.
